01 — About

A psychologist's lens on machine intelligence.

I work at the intersection of cognitive psychology and applied AI, training large-scale models to reason more accurately, communicate more empathetically, and serve under-represented language communities — particularly Indonesian.

My day-to-day spans Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback for both LLMs and Vision-Language Models — auditing Chain-of-Thought logic on edge cases, engineering negative-constraint prompts, and verifying spatial reasoning against ground truth. I currently train frontier models at Outlier (Scale AI) and evaluate UX research for global products.

Outside of evaluation work, I build agentic AI applications in Langflow — focused on healthcare and well-being — and lead community education on ethical generative AI as a former Google Student Ambassador.
